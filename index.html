About

A Bit About Me

I'm Joe — a developer and artist with over 25 years in the software industry. I've worked on AAA titles like Disney Infinity, Need for Speed: Most Wanted, and the BAFTA-nominated Pure, across roles spanning lead artist, principal technical artist, programmer, and designer.

These days I build interactive experiences for mobile and web and explore areas like machine learning and generative AI. I enjoy bridging the gap between art and technology — whether that's crafting game pipelines or training a GAN to generate faces.
